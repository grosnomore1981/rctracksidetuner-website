Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

RC Trackside Tuner respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the RC Trackside Tuner app handles information.

Information We Collect

RC Trackside Tuner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information from users.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its features.

Setup Notes and Local Data

RC Trackside Tuner includes a Setup Notes feature that allows users to record setup information, track conditions, results, and other notes.

Information entered into Setup Notes is stored locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to RC Trackside Tuner or to a remote server.

Because this information is stored locally, deleting the app or clearing its application data may permanently remove saved notes.

Analytics and Tracking

RC Trackside Tuner does not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or cross-app tracking technologies.

Advertising

RC Trackside Tuner does not display advertisements and does not collect information for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

RC Trackside Tuner does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the features or data practices of RC Trackside Tuner change. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
